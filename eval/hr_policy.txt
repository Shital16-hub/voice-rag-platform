HR POLICY DOCUMENT
==================

LEAVE POLICY
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.
Leave must be approved by the direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.
Leave encashment is not permitted except upon resignation.

SICK LEAVE POLICY
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
Work from home must be approved by the manager.
Employees must be available during core hours of 10am to 4pm.

BENEFITS
All full-time employees are entitled to health insurance coverage.
The company contributes 50% of the health insurance premium.
Employees can add dependents to their health insurance plan.

PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year in June and December.
Salary increments are based on performance ratings.
Employees rated below expectations for two consecutive reviews may face a performance improvement plan.
